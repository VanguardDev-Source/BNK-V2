## 🔫 Weapon Identification

Weapon serialization provides unique serial identification for applicable weapon instances.

### Platform Limitation

The GTA V / FiveM weapon environment does **not natively expose sufficient persistent metadata** to reliably associate every physical weapon instance with complete provenance information.

The following fields therefore cannot be reliably implemented as native weapon-instance attributes:

```text
Spawned By
Contrabanded By
Rewarded To
```

### Current Support

| Attribute | Status |
|---|---|
| Serial Number | ✅ Implemented |
| Spawned By | ⚠️ GTA/FiveM Environment Limitation |
| Contrabanded By | ⚠️ GTA/FiveM Environment Limitation |
| Rewarded To | ⚠️ GTA/FiveM Environment Limitation |

### Technical Reason

The underlying GTA V weapon environment does not provide a guaranteed native mechanism for permanently binding custom provenance metadata to an individual physical weapon throughout its complete lifecycle.

Server-side systems can maintain external records around inventory transactions, weapon events, and other application-level data; however, this does not provide the same guarantee as native persistent weapon-instance metadata.

The implementation therefore prioritizes **reliable serial identification** rather than exposing provenance information that cannot be guaranteed by the underlying game environment.

> **Conclusion:** The limitation is architectural to the GTA V / FiveM weapon environment and is not simply a missing configuration or resource setting.
