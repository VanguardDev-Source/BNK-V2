# 🩺 Health Status System — ✅ Completed

Updated the medical status handling to distinguish between head and body conditions.

### Head

```text
Head → Body Bag
```

Critical head status is handled through the Body Bag workflow.

### Body

```text
Body → Revive
```

Body status is handled through the Revive workflow.

### Bed Interaction

Corrected the treatment flow so players receiving the appropriate revive treatment are **not unnecessarily sent to the hospital bed**.

This separates:

- Body Bag
- Revive
- Hospital Bed

into distinct medical actions.

# 🧹 Full Character Reset / City Wipe — ✅ Completed

Performed the requested **full character reset**.

### Reset Scope

The reset was designed to remove existing player progression, including:

- Character records
- Cash
- Bank balances
- Inventory
- Items
- Weapons
- Vehicle ownership
- Properties
- Job-related character data
- Persistent character metadata
- Other character-linked progression

### Objective

The city was reset so existing players would not retain previous:

```text
Money
Items
Inventory
Progression
Character Data
```

### Data Integrity

The reset was treated as a full character-data operation rather than simply removing visible character entries.

This ensured that player data did not unintentionally survive through secondary persistent records.
